# Guiding Prompt — The Real Alpha

**What we're actually looking for:**

Not the infrastructure engineers at Google/Shopify.

**The niche sideprojects of Google engineers testing agent preferences.**

---

## The Pattern

Google engineers don't just build UCP at work. They:
- Test how agents actually choose products
- Build side projects to understand ranking
- Publish findings on LinkedIn/Twitter
- Share what actually works vs what Google says works

**That's the alpha. Not the spec. The empirical testing.**

---

## Who We're Looking For

### The Empirical Testers

People who:
- Run actual experiments with ChatGPT/Gemini shopping
- Measure what agents actually recommend
- Test feed attributes against real queries
- Publish results (not just opinions)

### The Hidden Practitioners

People who:
- Have 500-5000 followers (not 50K)
- Post about specific experiments, not theory
- Share actual numbers, not projections
- Are building tools to measure agent behavior

### The Side-Project Builders

Engineers who:
- Build tools to test agent preferences on weekends
- Open-source their measurement frameworks
- Share GitHub repos with actual experiment code
- Post about what they learned, not what they built

## What They're Building

Not:
- "How to optimize for AI search" (theory)
- "Agent commerce is the future" (hype)

But:
- "I tested 100 Merchant Center feeds and here's what agents actually picked"
- "I built a tool to measure AI impression share"
