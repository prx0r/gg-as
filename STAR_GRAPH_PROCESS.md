# The Star Graph Process — How to Find Alpha

**Date:** 2026-09-07

---

## The Process

```
1. SEED with high-signal engineers in the niche
2. COLLECT their stars (what they're watching)
3. FIND repos starred by 2+ seeds (convergence)
4. THOSE REPOS are the alpha — where infrastructure is converging
5. GO DEEPER — check who maintains those repos
6. REPEAT — their stars become new seeds
```

---

## Why This Works

**Stars = attention.**

When 3 smart people star the same repo, it means:
- They think this is important
- They're watching it closely
- They might build on top of it
- The repo is likely to ship something

**That's convergence without collaboration.**
